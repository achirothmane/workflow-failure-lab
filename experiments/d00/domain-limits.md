# D00 Domain Limits

## Included design-visible measurement domains

- **CI rerun / WFL**: eligible D01 owner-controlled rerun task.
- **Kubernetes node mutation / Aegis**: compatible with the same measurement dictionary for D02 observations, without granting new authority here.

## Not selected by D00

- no D03 held-out domain/cohort;
- no production target;
- no market/payment cohort;
- no new credential scope;
- no universal savings threshold.

## D03 blinding rule

D03 may reuse the measurement dictionary after independent cohort selection, but concrete held-out cases, domain oracle and adversarial schedules are not defined here and must not feed pretest kernel redesign.
